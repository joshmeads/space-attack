# Player fire cadence

- The single editable rate limit is `CONFIG.playerFireIntervalMs = 400`. `PLAYER_FIRE_INTERVAL_TICKS` derives the integer cooldown with `Math.ceil(milliseconds * CONFIG.tickRate / 1000)`, giving 24 ticks at 60 Hz. No separate editable tick value remains.
- This rate uses the canonical logical field, independent of resolution, aspect ratio or theme. A player bullet spawns at `212 - 3.5 - 3 = 205.5` and exits strictly below `-3`; the 208.5-unit flight takes `208.5 / 4.5 = 46.333...` ticks, requiring 47 discrete ticks. Two evenly spaced shots therefore need `ceil(47 / 2) = 24` ticks, or 400 ms.
- The two-bullet pool remains a safety bound. Hits, cancellation and released fire input do not reset or bypass cooldown. Existing hit-stop freezes cooldown with the rest of the simulation. Saved cooldowns up to the former 12-tick bound remain valid; persistence changes its bound to the derived export in its separate unit.
- Four regression cases cover held fire, repeated presses, enemy hits and bullet cancellation. Empty-lane firing produces shots at ticks 0, 24, 48, 72 and 96. A removed bullet is reused only after the remaining cooldown expires.
