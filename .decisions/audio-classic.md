# Classic generated audio

Classic adds the original sparse square-wave loop Signal Watch and eight compact ZZFX effects. Its music sits below the effects with quiet bass pulses and nearly silent percussion. Rest steps keep the loop unobtrusive, and the white player shot receives a brief descending square-wave sound. Dive, hit and death sounds use descending or crushed generated waveforms.

MusicScore now accepts bass rests and square-wave bass. Existing Retro and Modern score data and controller methods stay unchanged. All three synth sets and effect buffers are cached after the first player gesture. Classic uses the same immediate pause behavior as Retro. Theme switching changes playback without mutating the core.
