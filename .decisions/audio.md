- Music uses two original 64-step melodic scores composed in TypeScript: Orbital Patrol for retro and Afterburn Horizon for modern. Tone synthesizers and generated ZZFX buffers replace binary audio assets.
- AudioController creates and resumes a native AudioContext synchronously inside the first keyboard or touch handler, then dynamically imports Tone.js and ZZFX. Sharing that unlocked native context protects the initial iOS gesture while the modules download.
- ZZFX presets have zero pitch randomness and generate buffers once per theme. Music and effects have separate gain buses and preferences. Retro pause mutes immediately; modern pause fades over 120 milliseconds.
- Music tempo derives from wave number and the fraction of enemies defeated, with an upper bound to keep late-wave audio readable. Autonomous demo and benchmark callers must not feed their events to the player AudioController.
- Audio startup failure leaves the game playable and permits a later gesture to retry. Physical iOS Safari playback remains a device-specific verification requirement.
- A real headless Chromium smoke verified deferred library requests, a running unlocked context, live Tone transport notes, distinct music/SFX mute buses, immediate retro pause, modern fade, cached-theme switching, and tempo progression from 120 to 166 BPM. It reported zero page errors and zero console messages. Targeted Vite Plus lint/typecheck and TypeScript checks passed.
